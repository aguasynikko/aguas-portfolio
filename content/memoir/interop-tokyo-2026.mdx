On the 12th of June 2026 I was at Makuhari Messe for Interop Tokyo 2026, on an educational trip with my Mapúa classmates. The exhibition brought four shows together under one roof: Interop itself, Digital Signage Japan, the AI Native Expo and the Vision AI Expo. The theme was printed across the entrance banner in Japanese and English: *Internet for AI, AI for Internet.*

## The floor

The first thing that hits you is the scale. The halls are enormous, and every aisle is packed with booths, screens and demos running side by side.

Some of the booths I remember best were Cisco, Chrome Enterprise and Yamaha. Cisco was a name I already knew from studying for my CCNA certification, so it was strange and satisfying to see the company in person after spending so long with its networking material on a screen.

Almost every booth handed out something. By the end of the day we had collected bags, candies, bottles of water and even a case. It's a small detail, but it's part of what I remember about the day.

## What stayed with me

What amazed me most was how advanced everything was, especially the AI. It wasn't shown off as a separate product. It was built into networking equipment, into digital signage and into enterprise tools, which is the theme on the banner made real. As a student specializing in AI, I'm used to thinking about models on their own. Here I was seeing them as one part of much larger systems that companies are already selling.

The overseas pavilions also gave me the chance to meet professionals from other countries and swap ideas about where the technology is heading. I left Makuhari Messe with a bag full of souvenirs and a much clearer picture of where AI fits in the industry I'm about to join.
