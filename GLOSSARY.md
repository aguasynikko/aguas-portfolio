# Glossary

**Memoir**
The record of experiences outside the CV, such as events attended, papers presented and trips taken. It is made up of **Chapters**, written in first person and reflective in tone.
_Avoid_: blog, journal, casebook, news

**Chapter**
One essay in the Memoir, about a single experience. It has a number, a **Filed** date (when the experience happened) and a **Scene** (where it happened). Chapters are numbered oldest first.
_Avoid_: case, post, entry, article

**Case file**
The site's name for the Experience section (employment history). Not used for anything in the Memoir.
_Avoid_: using "case" for Chapters or Projects

**Exhibit**
A captioned photo placed inside a Chapter, lettered in order (Exhibit A, Exhibit B…).
_Avoid_: gallery image, attachment

**Post**
The original social-media announcement (usually on LinkedIn) that a Chapter is based on. It is a source the Chapter links back to; it is never the main text.
_Avoid_: chapter, entry

**Publication**
A peer-reviewed paper with a citation record (venue, DOI, authors). Presenting a Publication at a conference can be a Chapter; the Publication itself is not.

**Experience**
A period of employment, such as an internship, with a role, a company and dates.

**Project**
Something built, such as an app, model or dashboard, with its own stack, highlights and links.

## Relationships

- A **Chapter** may reference at most one **Publication**, **Experience** or **Project**, and that item links back to it.
- A **Chapter** may link to one **Post**.
- A **Chapter** holds zero or more **Exhibits**.

## Flagged ambiguities

- Project cards are labelled "Case 01, Case 02…", which overlaps with the **Case file** (Experience) section title. Unresolved.
